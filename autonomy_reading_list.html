01
Mathematical foundations
Partial
▼
Gold standard
Mathematics for Machine Learningfree Deisenroth, Faisal, Ong · 2020
Purpose-built bridge from ML to deeper math. Linear algebra, probability, and optimisation in unified notation. Free PDF at mml-book.github.io
Introduction to Linear Algebra Gilbert Strang · 5th ed
The MIT standard. If you want eigenvalue intuition and matrix decompositions from first principles, this is the one.
Hands-on
Kalman and Bayesian Filters in Pythonfree Roger Labbe · GitHub
Introduces the math by making you implement filters. Best way to make the Gaussian conditioning concrete. Every formula has a notebook cell.
Mathematics for ML Specialisationfree audit Imperial College · Coursera
Worked problems with graded exercises. Covers the three specific tools: linear algebra, multivariate calculus, PCA.
101
Essence of Linear Algebrafree 3Blue1Brown · YouTube
Visual geometric intuition for every operation. Watch this before reading any textbook. 15 videos, ~3 hours total.
No Bullshit Guide to Linear Algebra Ivan Savov · 2nd ed
Densely direct. No motivation, no padding, just the tools. Good if you want a quick reference map before going deep.
02
Probabilistic estimation
Priority next
▼
Gold standard
Probabilistic Robotics Thrun, Burgard, Fox · MIT Press 2005
THE book. Every autonomy engineer has read Ch.1–5. KF, EKF, particle filters, SLAM — all derived rigorously. Slightly dated on SLAM but foundational on estimation.
State Estimation for Roboticsfree Tim Barfoot · Cambridge 2017
The modern rigorous reference. Uses Lie groups (SO(3), SE(3)) for 3D estimation. Graduate level but the appendices alone are worth it. Free PDF from author.
Hands-on
Kalman and Bayesian Filters in Pythonfree Roger Labbe · github.com/rlabbe
14 Jupyter notebooks. Build every filter from scratch. The single best hands-on resource in this domain. Start here before any textbook.
filterpyfree Roger Labbe · Python library
Reference implementation. Read the source — it's cleaner than most textbook pseudocode. Useful when your Rust implementation diverges unexpectedly.
101
Understanding the Kalman Filterfree Faragher · IEEE Signal Proc. 2012
8-page paper. Best single document for getting the intuition. Read this first — it will make Thrun Ch.3 take 1 hour instead of 4.
Special Topics: The Kalman Filterfree Michel van Biezen · YouTube
55 short videos (5–8 min each). Extremely accessible, works through scalar examples before matrix form. Good for building mechanical fluency.
03
Perception & signal processing
Strong
▼
Gold standard
Multiple View Geometry in Computer Vision Hartley & Zisserman · Cambridge 2004
The geometric CV bible. Projective geometry, camera models, epipolar geometry, reconstruction. Required reading before visual odometry or SLAM.
Introduction to Radar Systems Skolnik · 3rd ed, McGraw-Hill
The standard radar reference. Range, Doppler, antenna patterns, clutter, CFAR. Your gap area. Ch.1–3 is enough to read Anduril's radar product literature fluently.
Hands-on
Think DSPfree Allen Downey · Green Tea Press
Build every DSP concept in Python. FFT, filtering, spectrograms, convolution. Free PDF. Good for filling signal processing gaps alongside your existing CV knowledge.
Programming Computer Vision with Pythonfree Jan Erik Solem · free PDF
Practical implementation focus. Feature detection, optical flow, 3D reconstruction. Bridges theory to OpenCV-level code.
101
Computer Vision: Algorithms and Applicationsfree Szeliski · 2nd ed, free at szeliski.org
The accessible modern reference. Covers everything from image formation to deep learning. Good for finding the topic you need then going to the dedicated textbook.
But what is the Fourier Transform?free 3Blue1Brown · YouTube
Visual intuition for frequency domain in 20 minutes. If your FFT intuition is shaky, watch this before any DSP textbook. Everything else builds on this visual.
04
Real-time systems
Starting
▼
Gold standard
Hard Real-Time Computing Systems Giorgio Buttazzo · 3rd ed, Springer
Comprehensive real-time theory. Task scheduling, priority inversion, deadline guarantees, resource access protocols. The graduate-level reference for anything with hard timing constraints.
Real-Time Systems Jane Liu · Prentice Hall 2000
The classic academic reference. Rate monotonic scheduling, EDF, schedulability analysis. Slightly dated on implementation but the theory is foundational and unchanged.
Hands-on
The Rust Embedded Bookfree Rust Embedded WG · docs.rust-embedded.org
Official guide to Rust on embedded/RTOS targets. no_std, memory-mapped registers, interrupt handlers, HAL abstraction. Start here for the Rust real-time path.
Embedded Rust in Action Carl Löndahl McNamara · Manning 2023
Project-based. Build actual embedded systems in Rust. More practical than the embedded book, less comprehensive. Good complement.
101
Introduction to Embedded Systemsfree Lee & Seshia · 2nd ed, leeseshia.org
The conceptual foundation. Models of computation, timing semantics, concurrency, state machines. Excellent for understanding why real-time systems are structured the way they are.
FreeRTOS Getting Startedfree Amazon · freertos.org/Documentation
Even if you use Rust not C, understanding FreeRTOS tasks, queues, and semaphores gives you the mental model. 30-minute read, permanent vocabulary.
05
Control theory
Not started
▼
Gold standard
Feedback Systemsfree Åström & Murray · caltech.edu/~murray/amwiki
The modern definitive reference. Used at Caltech, MIT, Stanford. Covers classical and modern control in unified framework. Free PDF. This is the one to own.
Data-Driven Science and Engineeringfree Brunton & Kutz · databookuw.com
Explicitly bridges control theory to ML/data-driven methods. DMD, SINDy, Koopman operators. Written for someone with your background. Free PDF.
Hands-on
Control Bootcampfree Steve Brunton · YouTube
30 short videos from PID through LQR, Kalman, and MPC. Code in MATLAB/Python alongside. Best systematic hands-on intro. Watch before opening any textbook.
Brian Douglas — Control Systemsfree Brian Douglas · YouTube
~100 videos covering every control concept with excellent animations. The Khan Academy of control theory. Especially good for Bode plots, stability margins, PID tuning intuition.
101
Modern Control Engineering Katsuhiko Ogata · 5th ed
The standard undergraduate textbook. Every control course uses this. Dense but complete. Good reference once you have the intuition from Brunton's videos.
MATLAB Tech Talks: Control Systemsfree MathWorks · YouTube
Short animated explainers for PID, transfer functions, frequency domain, state-space. Very accessible. Perfect if you want the concept in 5 minutes before going deep.
06
Planning & decision
Not started
▼
Gold standard
Planning Algorithmsfree LaValle · planning.cs.uiuc.edu
The comprehensive reference. Covers discrete search through sampling-based planning through trajectory optimisation. Free full PDF. Exhaustive but well-structured — use it as a reference not a cover-to-cover read.
Behavior Trees in Robotics and AIfree Colledanchise & Ögren · free from authors
The only dedicated BT textbook. Formal analysis, design patterns, relation to FSMs and STRIPS planning. Essential if you're building a BT executor in Rust.
Hands-on
Underactuated Roboticsfree Russ Tedrake · MIT OCW + Drake
Lecture notes + code using the Drake framework. Trajectory optimisation, LQR trees, MPC. Probably the best graduate-level planning course available free. Connects planning directly to control.
OMPL Tutorialsfree ompl.kavrakilab.org
The Open Motion Planning Library. RRT, PRM, and 20+ other algorithms with a unified interface. Working through the tutorials gives you hands-on coverage of the major planning families.
101
AI for Roboticsfree Sebastian Thrun · Udacity
Accessible intro to localisation, search, PID, SLAM. Not deep but gives a good mental map before going into LaValle. Thrun is one of the best explainers in the field.
AI: A Modern Approach — Ch. 3–4free excerpts Russell & Norvig · 4th ed
Search algorithms chapter: BFS, DFS, A*, heuristics. If you haven't done formal algorithms, read this before LaValle. Russell's explanations are exceptionally clear.
07
Mission & autonomy
Adjacent
▼
Gold standard
Multiagent Systemsfree Shoham & Leyton-Brown · masfoundations.org
The comprehensive multi-agent reference. Game theory, mechanism design, distributed constraint optimisation, auctions. Free PDF. The foundation for multi-vehicle task allocation.
Introduction to Autonomous Mobile Robots Siegwart, Nourbakhsh, Scaramuzza · MIT Press 2nd ed
The autonomous systems textbook. Localisation, mapping, navigation, architecture. Ties all the stack layers together in one physical system context. The survey book for the domain.
Hands-on
ROS2 + Nav2 Stackfree Open Source · docs.ros.org
Even for defence work, ROS2 is the best pedagogical environment to see all stack layers working together: DDS transport, behaviour trees, EKF localisation, A* planning, PID control. Install it and run Nav2 on a simulated robot — you'll see your whole landscape in one running system.
Programming Robots with ROS Quigley, Gerkey, Smart · O'Reilly 2015
Practical ROS guide with worked projects. Dated on ROS2 specifics but the architectural concepts are timeless. Good companion to the official docs.
101
Anduril Lattice talksfree Anduril Industries · YouTube
Search "Anduril Lattice architecture" on YouTube. Several public talks by their engineering leadership. Rare real-world glimpse into how a production mission system is actually designed. Watch this early to understand what you're building toward.
MIT 6.832 Underactuated Roboticsfree Russ Tedrake · MIT OCW
The full course bridges planning + control + dynamics + autonomy. The lecture on trajectory stabilisation connects everything. Best single course for seeing the full stack integrated.
